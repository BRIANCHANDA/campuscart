CampusCart
Campus shopping & delivery · Copperbelt University, Kitwe

High-fidelity mobile mockups across four roles — shopper, courier, shop admin and platform admin — built on the HealthChain token system (DM Sans, 8pt grid, restrained elevation) with a warm campus accent. Green anchors trust and primary actions; amber carries energy, prices and promos for bright-daylight readability. Prices in Zambian Kwacha, phones in +260 format.
